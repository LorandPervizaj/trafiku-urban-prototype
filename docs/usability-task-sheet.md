# Participant task sheet

Trafiku Urban / RIT Kosovo / ISTE 260 / Part 3

This is a student prototype. Vehicles, departure times and return-direction service are simulated. You do not need to buy a ticket or enter personal information. Please say what you are thinking as you work. The facilitator is testing the design, not you. You can stop at any time.

Participant code: ______    Date: ______    Device/browser: ______

1. Find a journey from Hotel Prishtina to Europe House. Choose a route you would inspect further. This geographically supported equivalent replaces the unverified Dardania-to-RIT scenario.
2. Find the boarding stop and direction. Explain what the app can and cannot confirm about the side of the street.
3. Read each departure state the facilitator presents: LIVE DEMO, SCHEDULED DEMO and UNAVAILABLE. Explain what each tells you and whether you would trust it for a real departure.
4. Save the journey, leave its details, and reopen it. Then remove the saved journey.
5. Find ticket information and open the official wallet website. Stop before registration or payment. Explain where the purchase happens and whether the displayed fares are current.
6. On the map, display only Line 4, hide stops, and return to all lines.

After each task: How easy was that? 1 Very difficult / 2 Difficult / 3 Neutral / 4 Easy / 5 Very easy.

Closing prompts: What was confusing? What would you change? What information did you expect but could not find?
