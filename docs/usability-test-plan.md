# Usability test plan

## Purpose and participants

Evaluate route discovery, boarding comprehension, reliability labels, recurring-trip access, ticket handoff and map filters with real potential users. Recruit students/part-time workers similar to Arta and regular commuters similar to Blerim. Use anonymous participant codes. Explain the prototype and obtain voluntary consent. Collect only the minimum information needed, such as transit frequency, approximate age band and device. Do not record identifying information without explicit consent.

## Repeatable setup

Serve the app over HTTP, open it on Map, and reset preferences through map settings. Keep the same default journey: Hotel Prishtina to Europe House, supported by the retained shared-stop dataset. Make internet available for tiles/wallet. Note device, browser, viewport and network. Testing controls allow the facilitator to reset simulation positions, clear saved trips and select LIVE, SCHEDULED or UNAVAILABLE. Reset before each participant. Test all three reliability states in a counterbalanced order where feasible; record the order. These states are demonstrations, never live municipal data.

Use usability-task-sheet.md with the participant. Begin timing when the participant starts each task, stop on success, abandonment or the preselected time limit (recommended 3 minutes). Do not coach except to prevent accidental personal data entry/payment. Record help separately. Operational definition of success must be fixed before sessions: correct route selected; stop/direction/side limitation stated; each status understood; trip saved and reopened; wallet reached; filter state achieved.

## Measures

Record completion (independent/with help/failed), task seconds, critical errors preventing completion or causing a wrong decision, noncritical errors recovered from, error-free completion, ease rating 1-5, confusing controls, participant comments and observer evidence. Define critical versus noncritical consistently before coding notes. Do not infer elapsed time after the session. Report sample size and missing observations; do not calculate percentages from missing or mixed-denominator rows without saying so.

## Analysis and reporting

Use observation-template.md and usability-report-template.md. Compare repeated issues against tasks/personas, distinguish observed behavior from interpretation, and prioritize by frequency and impact. Recommend specific changes and retest those tasks after revision. Keep quotes exact and anonymized. Small formative samples do not justify population-level claims.

No participants, sessions, completion scores or findings have been fabricated. These materials are ready for actual sessions; results remain blank until collected.
